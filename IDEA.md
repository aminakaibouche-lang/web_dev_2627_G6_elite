# HealPath — Pharmacy and Medicine Finder

## 1. Project Name and Description

**Project Name: Heal_Path**

**Slogan:** Find the right pharmacy at the right time with the medicine you need.

HEAL_PATH is a web platform designed to help people find pharmacies and medicines quickly and easily. It allows users to search for medicines, check their availability, find nearby pharmacies that are currently open, and identify pharmacies on night duty (*pharmacies de garde*). The platform also provides pharmacy contact information, locations, and profiles to help users save time and find the right pharmacy, especially when they need medicine outside normal working hours.

## 2. Problem Statement

Sometimes, finding a specific medicine or an available pharmacy can be difficult and time-consuming. People may visit several pharmacies before finding the medicine they need, without knowing whether it is in stock. This problem becomes more challenging at night, on weekends, and outside normal working hours, when people need to find a pharmacy that is open or on night duty.

FarmAway aims to solve this problem by providing a simple, centralized platform where users can search for medicines, check reported availability, find open and night-duty pharmacies, and access pharmacy contact information and locations.

### Target Users

- **Patients and general users:** People who need to find medicines and pharmacies quickly.
- **Pharmacists:** Pharmacy professionals who want to manage their pharmacy profiles, medicine information, and duty schedules.
- **Administrators:** People responsible for managing pharmacy records, medicine information, and user accounts on the platform.

## 3. Services

### 1. Medicine Search

Users can search for a specific medicine by its name to find pharmacies that report having it available.

### 2. Pharmacy Search

Users can search for pharmacies by name or location to find pharmacies in their area.

### 3. Medicine Availability

Users can check the reported availability of a medicine at a pharmacy before visiting, helping them avoid unnecessary trips.

### 4. Night-Duty Pharmacy Finder
Users can find pharmacies on night duty (*pharmacies de garde*) in a selected area, especially when they need medicine outside normal working hours.

### 5. Pharmacy Profile and Contact Information

Users can view pharmacy details, including its name, address, phone number, location, opening hours, and other available information.

### 6. Open Pharmacy Finder

Users can identify pharmacies that are currently open based on their published schedules and available status information.

## 4. User Profiles and Privileges

### Profile 1: Patient / Normal User

Patients and general users can access the public features of FarmAway.

**Privileges:**
- Search for medicines by name.
- Search for pharmacies by name or location.
- Check reported medicine availability.
- Find pharmacies that are currently open.
- Find pharmacies on night duty.
- View pharmacy profiles, addresses, locations, phone numbers, and opening hours.

### Profile 2: Pharmacist

Pharmacists can manage the information related to their own pharmacy through a dedicated dashboard.

**Privileges:**
- Log in to their pharmacist account.
- View and update their pharmacy profile.
- Manage their pharmacy's medicine list and reported availability.
- Update pharmacy contact information and opening hours.
- Manage or submit updates to their night-duty schedule, according to the platform's rules.
- Log out securely.

### Profile 3: Administrator

Administrators manage the platform and oversee its information and registered accounts.

**Privileges:**
- Log in to the administration dashboard.
- Add, edit, and manage pharmacy records.
- Manage medicine records.
- Manage pharmacy duty schedules.
- Manage pharmacist accounts and permissions.
- Review and correct pharmacy information to help maintain data quality.
- Log out securely.

## 5. Main Website Pages

The planned website will include the following pages:

1. **Home Page:** Introduces FarmAway and provides access to the main search services.
2. **Medicine Search Page:** Allows users to search for medicines and check reported availability.
3. **Night-Duty Pharmacy Page:** Helps users find pharmacies on duty in their selected area.
4. **Pharmacy Search Page:** Allows users to find pharmacies by name or location.
5. **Pharmacy Details Page:** Displays the selected pharmacy's profile, contact details, location, opening hours, and available information.
6. **Login Page:** Allows registered pharmacists and administrators to access their accounts.
7. **Registration Page:** Allows authorized users to register according to the platform's account rules.
8. **Pharmacist Dashboard:** Allows pharmacists to manage their own pharmacy information, medicine availability, and duty schedules.
9. **Administrator Dashboard:** Allows administrators to manage pharmacies, medicines, duty schedules, and user accounts.
